  the `scale` columns are each engine against its own one-
  connection rate. This is the weakest row of the report and the
  difference is architectural: ckdbs dispatches one core's
  statements on one thread, so its curve is round-trip overlap;
  PostgreSQL runs a backend process per connection, so its curve
  is parallel execution. Both are being reported correctly.
